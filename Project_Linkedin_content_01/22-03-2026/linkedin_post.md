# LinkedIn All-Star Profile: The 7 Sections Every QA Needs

11x. That is how much more likely you are to be found by hiring managers with just one change.

It is not your framework choice or your years of experience.
It is your profile photo.

I watched a senior SDET struggle for 6 months to get a single interview.
His resume was perfect. His GitHub was active.
But his LinkedIn profile was "Incomplete."

He had no professional headshot and only 12 connections.
In the eyes of the LinkedIn algorithm, he did not exist.

Here is the 7-step checklist I gave him to reach "All-Star" status (and get noticed):

1. **Professional Photo**: Use a high-quality headshot. It is the first thing they see.
2. **The Result-Driven Headline**: Don't just list your title. Use this: "SDET Manager | AI & LLM Test Architect | Helping QA Engineers Thrive in the AI Era."
3. **The "Builder" Summary**: Focus on the problems you solve, not just the tools you use.
4. **Deep Experience**: You need at least 3 job descriptions with full details. Add all 5 roles from your resume.
5. **Education**: Be specific. (e.g., NSIT Delhi, B.Tech).
6. **The Skill Stack**: List at least 5. For modern QA, prioritize: Playwright, Selenium, AI Testing, and CI/CD.
7. **The 50+ Rule**: You need at least 50 connections to unlock All-Star status. Sync your email and connect with former colleagues.

The tools are not the hurdle. The visibility is.

Your profile is either a magnet or a ghost town.
